# Morph: Shapeshifter (Minecraft Bedrock add-on)

You don't have a normal body. You're a faceless shapeshifter: a thin shadow figure with glowing violet veins and two eye slits. When you kill a mob, you rip its skin off and pull it into yourself, and then you **become that mob**. You get its body, its hand in first person and its powers.

## Install (phone / tablet)

1. Download **`dist/Morph.mcaddon`** to your phone.
2. Tap it. Minecraft opens and imports both packs.
3. Create or edit a world. Go to **Behavior Packs**, turn on **Morph: Shapeshifter (Behavior)**, and the resource pack turns on with it.
4. You need **Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.30 or newer**. No experimental toggles are needed.

When you join, you get a **Morph Core** (a glowing purple orb).

## Controls (touch-friendly)

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Kill a mob** | Steals its skin and turns you into it |
| **Use (long-press) the Morph Core** | Uses your current form's ability |
| **Sneak + use the Morph Core** | Opens the morph menu: pick any skin you've stolen, toggle auto-morph, or see form info |

Commands are also available: `/scriptevent morph:menu` opens the menu and `/scriptevent morph:unlock_all` unlocks every skin (for testing).

## The skin-steal animation

1. The mob dies. A ghostly purple copy of its skin peels off the corpse.
2. The skin spins and crumples as it arcs through the air toward you. It sheds flakes in the mob's colours.
3. You're lifted off the ground with your arms spread, shaking, inside a purple vortex. Your body glows and pulses, and your first-person hand trembles.
4. On impact the screen flashes purple, particles burst out and your body swaps to the new mob. A **SKIN STOLEN** title appears.

## How it works

* `packs/MorphBP/entities/player.json` is the vanilla player with two synced entity properties added: `morph:form` and `morph:absorb`.
* `packs/MorphRP/entity/player.entity.json` is the vanilla player client entity. All vanilla animations are kept (walking, attacking, items, first-person). The skin and model are swapped for the ones in `controller.render.morph.third_person` and `controller.render.morph.first_person`, chosen by `morph:form`.
* First person uses its own small model per form (`morph_first_person.geo.json`). Each one is just that mob's arm (or leg, rod, etc.) placed where the player's arm normally is, so vanilla first-person animations line up.
* `packs/MorphBP/scripts/main.js` handles everything else: detecting kills, the steal sequence, abilities and the menu. It uses `@minecraft/server` 2.4.0 and `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.0.0.
* Mob forms use the game's own mob textures, so they always match your game version. The shapeshifter texture, icons and particles are generated by `tools/generate_assets.py`.

Rebuild after editing:

```sh
python3 tools/generate_assets.py   # textures + models
python3 tools/build.py             # -> dist/Morph.mcaddon
```

## Known limitations

* As in vanilla, your first-person hand only shows when your main hand is empty. When you hold an item, you see the item.
* Armor is drawn for a human shape, so on non-humanoid forms (creeper, spider, blaze) it floats oddly.
* Your hitbox and camera height stay player-sized in every form.
* This pack replaces the player entity files, so it will conflict with other add-ons that also replace `player.json` or `player.entity.json`.
